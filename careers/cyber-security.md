# Cyber Security Jobs

In whatever field you go on; you can see one common pattern. Most of the public try to go to a few famous job profiles and that too mostly for fame. But, it can easily be said that the main reason for such a pattern would be a shortage of knowledge regarding different profiles available.
And, CyberSecurity is also no different there are many different profiles that you can work within this but most people want to be "PenTester". Many times even you would see people demean and overlook other profiles without whom everything can fall apart. Hence, there is no profile better or worse to work in as they are as symbiotic.
Now, let's look at different work profiles available many of these could be a good option for you are switching from other fields or comfortable to something already.
